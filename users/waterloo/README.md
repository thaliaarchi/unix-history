# Sources for the University of Waterloo and UNIX

## J. Wesley Graham papers

University of Waterloo Archives \
[Accession GA133 - J. Wesley Graham fonds, 1946-1999](https://archives.uwaterloo.ca/j-wesley-graham-fonds) \
File 1529 UNIX, 1974-1977

The entirety of file 1529 is available on the [Internet Archive](https://archive.org/details/@archibits?query=subject%3A%22University+of+Waterloo+Archives%22).

- [University of Manitoba UNIX software agreement](https://archive.org/details/manitoba_unix_license_1975),
  effective 1 February 1975

  A software agreement for the UNIX Time-Sharing System for the University of
  Manitoba, effective 1 February 1975, and correspondence between R. Collens,
  University of Manitoba and Richard G. Shahpazian, Western Electric. The
  distribution had a service charge of $150.

  Also listed for the [University of Manitoba](../manitoba/README.md).

- [UNIX News July 30 1975](https://archive.org/details/unix_news_july-30-1975_waterloo)

  This scan is clearer than the copy available from the [USENIX Archives](https://archive.org/details/unix_news_july-30-1975),
  especially for the mailing list, and still has the subscription mail-back
  form.

- [Suggestion for a C compiler with WATFOR-like diagnostics](https://archive.org/details/waterloo_c_compiler_proposal_1975),
  15 December 1975

  A suggestion from Eric Manning to Wes Graham, dated 15 December 1975, for a C
  compiler for UNIX with superb error diagnostics like the WATFOR Fortran
  compiler and that's faster. It was declined due to limited resources at the
  time.

- [UNIX News February 10 1976](https://archive.org/details/unix_news_feb-10-1976_waterloo)

  This scan is clearer than the copy available from the [USENIX Archives](https://archive.org/details/unix_news_feb-10-1976),
  with fully readable text on page 8 and the missing page 9.

- [WATFOR for UNIX](https://archive.org/details/watfor_for_unix_1976-04-30),
  30 April 1976

  Letter from Wes Graham, University of Waterloo to John Lions, University of
  New South Wales on 30 April 1976 notifying that they had developed a version
  of WATFOR for UNIX.

- [University of Waterloo request for UNIX](https://archive.org/details/waterloo_unix_request_1976-06-16),
  16 July 1976

  Letter from K.I. McPhee, University of Waterloo to Irma Biren, Bell
  Laboratories on 16 July 1976 requesting a copy of the UNIX Time-Sharing System
  for the Computer Systems Group, independent of other departments at the
  university which run UNIX.

- [University of Waterloo membership in UNIX User's Group](https://archive.org/details/waterloo_unix_users_group_membership_1977),
  14 March and 14 April 1977

  The membership fee for the University of Waterloo joining the UNIX Users'
  Group and a request for Harvard's MACRO-11 package. Correspondence between
  Michael T. O'Brien at the University of Illinois at Chicago Circle, F. David
  Boswell at the University of Waterloo, and Melvin Ferentz at Brooklyn College
  of CUNY on 14 March and 14 April 1977.
